W Floor™ and the BRUTE™ drive

A moving floor built for waste trucks. Leachate from wet waste stays in the box instead of dripping all along the road.

Ask about the W Floor

Leak-tight across the full length and width

With the W Floor, HALLCO makes the subdeck part of the floor system and lays the load-bearing aluminium slats on top. The result is a leak-tight floor across the entire length and width of the trailer that holds liquids in, which is common when hauling household waste.

  • Full 1/2 inch (12.7 mm) aluminium slats for heavy loads
  • Full-length bearings for smooth, even operation
  • Each slat removable with just four bolts
  • Field repairs with common parts
  • Design protected by US patent no. 8,887,896

BRUTE™: the front-mounted drive

The W Floor is designed to work with the 8000 series, called BRUTE. Its three-cylinder hydraulic unit sits at the front of the box and connects to the slats under a sloped, hinged cover that opens for inspection. The trailer only needs a small modification around the front kingpin area.

According to HALLCO, mounting it at the front gives you:

  • Hydraulics mounted high, away from road salt and dirt
  • Drive weight moved towards the tractor, for more payload
  • No parts or hydraulic hoses under the trailer
  • Faster installation
  • Front access for service, repair or replacement

When to choose a different floor

Not every truck carrying damp cargo needs a fully sealed floor.

  • CompostHALLCO recommends the i-4000 with NLR slats to resist leaks.
  • Recyclablesi-4000 with NLR-HD slats, which hold a small amount of liquid.
  • Scrapi-4000 with Tufdeck, which also holds a small amount of liquid.
  • Dry wastei-4000 or i-6000 with Triple Ridge, depending on payload.

The warranty document Như Tín holds covers the i-Series. Warranty terms for the W Floor and BRUTE will be confirmed with the factory and stated in your quote.
