A moving floor for every bulk cargo

According to HALLCO, a LIVE FLOOR® moves any bulk material with enough weight on the floor and enough friction. What you choose is the slat profile and drive that suit that cargo.

  • Wood chips
  • Sawdust
  • Rice husk
  • Cashew nut shells

Wood chips

Wood chips travel long distances from chipping yards to ports or to pellet, paper and boiler plants. The cargo is light and bulky, so trucks usually carry high-sided boxes, and tipping under a warehouse roof or next to power lines is difficult.

With a moving floor, the truck backs into the yard, opens the rear door and unloads on the spot, even indoors. The clean-out tarp sweeps up the last fine chips at the end of the box.

HALLCO suggested configuration
i-4000 or i-6000 with Standard slats, drive chosen by payload.

Sawdust

Sawdust is fine, light and easily airborne. When tipped, dust rises with the whole load as it falls. A moving floor pushes the cargo out slowly at floor height, so there is less dust and the pile is easier to control.

Fine particles can pass through the gaps between slats. Như Tín considers the slat profile and a clean-out tarp for this cargo during the survey.

HALLCO suggested configuration
Wood group: i-4000 or i-6000 with Standard slats. Như Tín confirms the profile after seeing a sample.

Rice husk

Rice husk is a common boiler fuel in the Mekong Delta and other rice regions. It is very light, so each trip needs a large box, and trucks often unload in the plant's covered store.

A level box lets the truck work in low buildings. Because husk is light, the pressure on the floor is low, so the slat profile has to be chosen carefully.

Suggested configuration
Close to the produce group (i-4000 with AG Topseal). Như Tín confirms after seeing a sample and the truck.

Cashew nut shells

This is Như Tín's own core cargo: cashew nut shell residue left after oil pressing, delivered by our dedicated fleet to industrial boilers.

Hauling and unloading this very cargo is why Như Tín can advise customers in the same industry from real experience, not just from a lookup table.

Suggested configuration
HALLCO has no published configuration for cashew shells. Như Tín advises from hands-on operating experience.

Produce: corn, cassava

Grain corn, corn silage, dried cassava chips and other produce move with the harvest, in large volumes over a short time. A truck that unloads quickly and by itself at many receiving points is a big advantage at peak season.

With grain, what matters is that nothing leaks through the gaps or is lost on the road.

HALLCO suggested configuration
i-4000 with AG Topseal slats to limit product loss.

Animal feed and ingredients

Dried distillers grain, bran, oilseed meal and other bulk ingredients for feed mills. The HALLCO photo here shows dried distillers grain (DDG) being unloaded from a trailer.

Powders and small grains need tightly sealed slats, and the box must unload cleanly so loads don't mix between trips.

HALLCO suggested configuration
Grain group: i-4000 with AG Topseal. A clean-out tarp can be added for a clean box.

Household waste

Household waste in Vietnam is often wet, with a lot of leachate. A truck that is not sealed drips leachate along the road, causing odour and complaints. A moving floor trailer hauls large volumes from transfer stations to treatment sites and unloads at the tip face without raising the box.

HALLCO suggested configuration
Wet waste: BRUTE 8500 with the leak-tight W Floor. Dry waste: i-4000 or i-6000 with Triple Ridge.

Scrap and recyclables

Used tyres, scrap metal, plastics and recycled paper. The cargo has sharp edges, lands hard on the floor during loading and sometimes contains liquid.

HALLCO suggested configuration
Recyclables: i-4000 with NLR-HD. Scrap: i-4000 with Tufdeck or Triple Ridge. Paper and cardboard: i-4000 or i-6000 with Triple Ridge.

Compost

Composting feedstock and finished organic fertiliser are usually damp and heavy with some leachate, though less than household waste.

HALLCO suggested configuration
i-4000 with NLR slats to resist leaks.

Coal

Fine and lump coal for boilers and kilns. It is heavy, so each trip carries a high payload, the drive needs enough force and the box must stay within its rated load.

Suggested configuration
HALLCO has no published configuration for coal. Như Tín chooses the drive by actual payload, usually considering the i-6000 for heavy loads.
