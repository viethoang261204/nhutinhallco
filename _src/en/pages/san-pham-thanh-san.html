NLR-HD: thick enough to take the impact

Recyclables and scrap often have sharp edges and land hard on the floor during loading. HALLCO made the NLR-HD thicker to absorb impact while still limiting product loss.

  • Triple-ridge profile for durability
  • 22 mm thick
  • Four vertical ribs for a stiff floor with less twist
  • High-grade aluminium alloy
  • A 21-slat kit fits most trailers
  • 3.6 m long bearings that cover more area

Replacing slats? Take three measurements

When ordering replacement slats for a HALLCO floor in service, measure three dimensions of the old slat as HALLCO describes:

  1. Leg to leg: the distance between the two legs under the slat.
  2. Thickness: the thickness of the slat top.
  3. Top width: measured across the top of the slat.

Include the serial number and model of the drive. The serial plate is on the rear base manifold, in the middle, or where the switching valve mounts. If the plate is missing, send Như Tín photos of the drive, the floor and the part you need.
