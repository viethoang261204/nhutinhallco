HALLCO® products

A moving floor is not an off-the-shelf box. It is a combination of drive, bearings, subdeck and slats, chosen for the cargo and for the truck that will carry it.

Four product groups

  • i-Series drives

    i-4000 and i-6000: six cylinders, mounted under the floor, seals enclosed inside the cylinders. Rated 35 and 45 tons (US).

    See the i-Series

  • W Floor and BRUTE

    A sealed floor for waste trucks, paired with the BRUTE drive mounted at the front of the box, with no hydraulic hoses under the trailer.

    See the W Floor

  • Floor slats and plastic floor

    Standard, LoadKeeper, Double Ridge, Triple Ridge, AG Topseal, Tufdeck, NLR, NLR-HD and the plastic slat. Each profile suits a cargo group.

    See the slat profiles

  • Stationary systems

    LIVE FLOOR® for bunkers, bins and containers fixed at the plant, feeding material steadily into the production line.

    See stationary systems
